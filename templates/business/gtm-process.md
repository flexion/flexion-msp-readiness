## Sales Process

### Sales Methodology
**Framework:** [e.g., MEDDIC, Challenger Sale, Consultative Selling]

### Sales Stages and Process

#### 1. Lead Qualification (MQL → SQL)
**Criteria for Sales-Qualified Lead (SQL):**
- [ ] Fits ICP (company size, industry, AWS usage)
- [ ] Has defined pain point related to AWS management
- [ ] Has budget or ability to purchase ($[X]K+ annual)
- [ ] Timeline to decision (< 90 days preferred)
- [ ] Access to decision maker

**Qualification Process:**
- Marketing automation scoring: [X] points threshold
- SDR qualification call (15 min)
- Handoff to Account Executive if qualified

#### 2. Discovery (Stage 1)
**Objective:** Understand customer environment, pain points, and goals

**Activities:**
- [ ] Discovery call with technical and business stakeholders
- [ ] AWS environment assessment (account structure, workloads, spend)
- [ ] Pain point identification and quantification
- [ ] Initial fit assessment

**Duration:** 1-2 weeks  
**Exit Criteria:** Mutual agreement to move to proposal stage

#### 3. Solution Design and Proposal (Stage 2)
**Objective:** Develop tailored MSP service package and proposal

**Activities:**
- [ ] Technical deep dive (architecture review)
- [ ] Service scope definition (SLA requirements, coverage hours, services included)
- [ ] Pricing proposal development
- [ ] ROI / business case creation
- [ ] Proposal presentation

**Duration:** 2-3 weeks  
**Exit Criteria:** Proposal accepted in principle, moving to negotiation

#### 4. Negotiation and Procurement (Stage 3)
**Objective:** Finalize contract terms and pricing

**Activities:**
- [ ] Contract review (MSA, SOW)
- [ ] Security and compliance review
- [ ] Legal and procurement engagement
- [ ] Reference calls (if requested)
- [ ] Final pricing negotiation

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks  
**Exit Criteria:** Signed contract

#### 5. Onboarding (Stage 4 - Post-Sale)
**Objective:** Successful customer onboarding and service delivery initiation

**Activities:**
- [ ] Kickoff meeting
- [ ] AWS account access setup (IAM roles, permissions)
- [ ] Tooling integration (monitoring, ITSM, alerting)
- [ ] Runbook development
- [ ] 30-day check-in

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks  
**Exit Criteria:** Customer live on managed services, first invoice sent

## Notes for AWS MSP Program Submission

**What AWS is Looking For:**
- **Structured Process:** Do you have repeatable, documented processes for demand gen and sales?
- **Enablement:** Are your sellers trained on AWS and your MSP offering?
- **Pipeline Health:** Do you have active pipeline indicating growth potential?
- **AWS Alignment:** Are you leveraging AWS partner programs (co-sell, Marketplace)?

**You Should Demonstrate:**
- Clear target market and ICP definition
- Multiple demand generation channels (not just referrals)
- Documented sales process with defined stages
- Sales training program exists
- Sales collateral and tools available
- Active AWS co-sell and partner engagement
- Pipeline metrics and reporting in place
- Evidence of recent wins and growth (ties to BUS-002)
