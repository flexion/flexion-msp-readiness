## Financial Review Process

### Review Cadence and Participants

#### Monthly Financial Review
- **Frequency:** Monthly (within 5 business days of month close)
- **Participants:** CFO, MSP Practice Lead, Finance Manager
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **Agenda:**
  - Review P&L vs budget and forecast
  - Analyze variance and trends
  - Review customer metrics (adds, churn, expansion)
  - Update rolling forecast
  - Identify risks and opportunities

#### Quarterly Business Review (QBR)
- **Frequency:** Quarterly (within 10 business days of quarter close)
- **Participants:** Executive team, Board (optional), Department heads
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Agenda:**
  - Quarterly financial results
  - Year-to-date performance vs plan
  - Strategic initiatives progress
  - Updated annual forecast
  - Budget reallocation if needed
  - Investment decisions

#### Annual Planning Review
- **Frequency:** Annually (Q4)
- **Participants:** Executive team, Department heads, Board
- **Duration:** Half-day session
- **Agenda:**
  - Prior year retrospective
  - Strategic plan for next 3 years
  - Annual budget development
  - Resource allocation
  - Goal setting and OKRs

### Review Outputs
<!-- What artifacts are produced from financial reviews? -->

- [ ] Monthly P&L Report with variance analysis
- [ ] Executive Dashboard (KPIs and trends)
- [ ] Updated Forecast (rolling 12-month)
- [ ] Board Report (for QBR)
- [ ] Action Items and Decisions Log

## Budget Management and Controls

### Budget Approval Process
1. **Budget Proposal:** Practice lead develops budget with finance
2. **Department Review:** Review with department heads for alignment
3. **Executive Approval:** CFO and CEO review and approve
4. **Board Approval:** [If applicable] Board approves annual budget
5. **Distribution:** Approved budget shared with budget owners

### Variance Management
**Variance Threshold:** Variances >10% or >$[X] require explanation and action plan

**Variance Response Process:**
1. Identify variance in monthly review
2. Root cause analysis (why did it occur?)
3. Action plan developed (how to address?)
4. Forecast update (does this change full-year outlook?)
5. Approval if reallocation needed (CFO approval for >$[X])

### Budget Change Control

## Notes for AWS MSP Program Submission

**What AWS is Looking For:**
- **Process Rigor:** Do you have a structured financial planning process?
- **Business Health:** Is your MSP practice financially sustainable and growing?
- **Discipline:** Are you tracking metrics and managing to a plan?
- **Maturity:** Do you have forecasts, budgets, and regular reviews?

**You Don't Need to Share:**
- Actual revenue/profit numbers (can use percentages or ranges)
- Sensitive competitive information
- Detailed customer pricing

**You Should Demonstrate:**
- Regular financial reviews occur (monthly/quarterly)
- Budgets are created and tracked
- Forecasts are updated based on actuals
- Key metrics are monitored (MRR, churn, margins, etc.)
- Financial planning drives decision-making
