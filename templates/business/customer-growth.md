## Notes for AWS MSP Program Submission

1. **Customer Privacy:** You may anonymize customer names if needed for privacy/NDA reasons. Use descriptive identifiers like "Financial Services Customer A"

2. **Contract Evidence:** AWS will want to see actual signed contracts or addenda. Ensure you have:
   - Clear dates (within last 18 months)
   - Explicit reference to managed services (not just project work)
   - Signatures or electronic acceptance records

3. **What Counts as "Growth":**
   - ✅ New customer signing MSP contract
   - ✅ Existing project-based customer adding MSP contract/addendum
   - ✅ Contract expansion significantly increasing scope (case-by-case)
   - ❌ Simple contract renewals without expansion
   - ❌ One-time project engagements without ongoing managed services

4. **Minimum Requirement:** At least 4 qualifying new contracts or addenda in the last 18 months demonstrating active growth of your MSP practice.
